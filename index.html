About PSPIFAAN

The POP Screeding Painting and Interior Finishing Artisan Association of Nigeria (PSPIFAAN) brings together artisans in POP ceiling installation, wall screeding, painting, interior finishing and related trades.

Our aims include promoting quality workmanship, professional conduct, skills development, and helping clients identify trustworthy artisans.

Official membership approval follows the association's constitution and established procedures.

For members

Digital registration, membership status, announcements, training information and access to opportunities.

For clients

A searchable directory of approved association members, clear contact preferences and a defined complaints process.
