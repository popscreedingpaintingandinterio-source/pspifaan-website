About PSPIFAAN

POP Screeding Painting and Interior Finishing Artisan Association of Nigeria

Our purpose

Promote workmanship, professional development, responsible practice and opportunities for artisans in POP, screeding, painting and interior finishing.

For members

Digital registration, membership status, announcements, training information and access to opportunities.

For clients

A searchable directory of approved association members, clear contact preferences and a defined complaints process.

Trust and verification

Association membership should not be described as government certification. Public badges should be shown only after the association completes its documented checks. The live site will need a privacy notice, terms, secure hosting, backups and a process for correcting or removing public profile information.
